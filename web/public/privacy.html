Family Dashboard privacy policy

Family Dashboard is a private, personal app used by one household to display its own Google Calendar on a screen at home.

  • It requests read-only access to one Google Calendar (calendar.readonly).
  • It reads event titles, times, locations and color labels only to display them on the household's own screens.
  • It does not write to the calendar, sell or share data, use it for advertising, or show it to anyone outside the household.
  • Events are cached in memory for about a minute and are not stored elsewhere.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
